[Project]

An archive for our monthly calls.

Five former MA students, five cities, one video call a month. Before each call everyone shares a selfie, answers a few questions and uploads photos and links. Nothing can be seen until the call begins. Then it all unlocks at once, becomes a shared presentation, and stays in the archive for good.

[The idea]

After the MA we scattered across the world. A monthly call keeps us together, but the conversations disappear as soon as we hang up.

The archive gives each call a small ritual. In the days before it, everyone quietly prepares their contribution. At the agreed time the site unlocks for everybody simultaneously, and the call opens with a presentation of what each person has been up to.

Over the months it builds into a record of where we were, what we were looking at and what we were thinking about: a shared diary written five ways.

[How it works]

  1. The admin opens a call

    They set the unlock time and write the questions. Each question is text, photo or link, required or optional, single or several. The selfie is a default question in every call.

  2. Everyone contributes

    Members log in with a one-time email link or code. Answers save automatically as a draft; Submit marks them as done, and everything stays editable until the unlock. A pop-up and a red notice remind anyone who hasn’t submitted yet.

  3. Nothing is visible until the call

    Before the unlock, the others see only who is ready: ● submitted, ◐ draft, ○ not started. The lock is enforced by the database itself, so not even the admin can look early.

  4. Call day becomes a show

    When the countdown reaches zero the site asks whether everyone is connected. The presentation picks people in a random order, revealed with a name roulette, and scrolls horizontally through each person’s selfie, photos and answers. Watching together, one click moves everyone on to the next person.

  5. It all stays in the archive

    Every call keeps its own page, and the materials (selfies, photos, texts and links) can be browsed across all calls and filtered by person and year.

[Details]

  • Timed unlockEnforced by row-level security in the database and storage, not by hiding things in the page.
  • Passwordless loginOne-time link or 6-digit code by email, sent only to the five members’ addresses.
  • Light photosResized in the browser before upload: a 1.1 MB photo becomes about 150 KB, plus a 20 KB thumbnail.
  • PrivacyLocation and other metadata are stripped from every photo. Files sit in a private bucket behind signed URLs.
  • Drafts and SubmitAutosave as you type; required answers are checked, with clear errors, before submitting.
  • Shared presentationSame random order for everyone; in “together” mode the person on screen is synced live.
  • Every timezoneDates and countdowns are shown in each viewer’s local time.
  • AccessibleWCAG 2.1 AA contrast, keyboard navigation, screen-reader labels and reduced-motion support, checked with axe.
